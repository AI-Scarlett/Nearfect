Purchase or unlock issue

If playstyle credits or the Everything Pack do not appear immediately, restart the app and make sure iCloud is enabled for your Apple ID. The Everything Pack can also be restored from the app settings. For consumable credit unlock issues, contact support with your App Store receipt information.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. If rankings or achievements are unavailable, check that you are signed into Game Center on your device.
