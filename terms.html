Gameplay

Nearfect! is a casual precision arcade game. The game can be played offline, although optional Apple services such as Game Center, App Store purchases, and iCloud sync require Apple's systems and an eligible Apple ID.

In-App Purchases

Playstyle credit purchases are consumable in-app purchases. Each credit pack permanently unlocks the next locked premium playstyles in the app. These unlocks are recorded in local game state and, when available, Apple's iCloud Key-Value Store. The Everything Pack is a non-consumable in-app purchase that permanently unlocks all current and future playstyles and can be restored through Apple.
